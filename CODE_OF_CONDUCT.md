# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Make room for newcomers and different communication styles.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Follow project security and licensing rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Knowingly exposing credentials, vulnerabilities, or user data.
- Impersonating maintainers or presenting an unofficial build as official.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate this policy. They may issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant,
depending on severity and pattern.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private reporting
channel or Security Advisory facility. If no private channel is available,
open a minimal public issue asking a maintainer to establish private contact;
do not include sensitive details in that issue.

Maintainers receiving a report must protect the reporter's privacy, review the
matter without conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome on a
best-effort basis.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly
represents the project or its community.
